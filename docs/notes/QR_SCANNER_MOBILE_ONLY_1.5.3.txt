ST Asset Management System - QR Scanner Mobile-Only Fix

Base version: ST Asset Management 1.5.3 Compact Tables
Scope: QR Scanner behavior only.

Changes:
- QR Scanner is shown in the sidebar only when the browser user-agent is detected as a mobile device.
- Direct desktop access to frontend/pages/qr_scanner.php shows a simple mobile-only notice instead of loading the camera scanner.
- The QR scanner itself remains unchanged from the current QR scanner implementation.
- No database changes are required.

Important localhost note:
- If the application is truly hosted only at http://localhost/st on a Windows PC,
  a separate phone cannot reach that PC through localhost. The mobile scanner UI
  will appear when the app is opened on a mobile browser, but the mobile browser
  must be able to reach the ST server before camera scanning can open asset pages.
